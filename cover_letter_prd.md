## Summary of results

The paper presents a phenomenological model of the fermion sector 
of the Standard Model based on modular A₄ symmetry on a 
T²/ℤ₃ orbifold. The main results are:

### 1. Strictly derived from first principles

- Bulk coupling constant: **α_G = 2/(3π) = 0.212207**
- Six-dimensional scale: **M₆ = 2.965×10¹⁶ GeV**, which 
  coincides with the GUT scale — a consequence of the 
  structure α_G = 2/(3π).

This is the central rigorous result of the work.

### 2. Reproduced observables

- **PMNS parameters:** all 4 parameters reproduced with 
  accuracy better than 0.5%.
- **Quark sector:** 11 parameters reproduce 9 observables 
  with χ² ~ 10⁻¹⁶.
- **Fine-tuning hierarchy:** Δ ~ 10⁵·¹³, comparable to the 
  Standard Model hierarchy y_t/y_u ~ 10⁵.

### 3. Conditional predictions

Given the phenomenological suppression factor 
ε = 2.860924×10⁻⁴, we obtain:

- Axion constant: f_a = 1.993×10¹¹ GeV
- Axion mass: m_a = 28.22 μeV
- Axion frequency: **ν_a = 6.824 GHz**, within the ADMX 
  search range (1–10 GHz).

### 4. Dark matter compatibility

The axion produces the observed dark matter density 
Ω_a h² = 0.12 at a natural O(1) misalignment angle 
θ_i = 1.112, consistent with Planck 2018.

## Honest assessment of limitations

We explicitly state in the manuscript the following limitations:

1. Only α_G is derived from first principles. All other 
   parameters are phenomenological.
2. ε is a phenomenological input — analytical expression 
   through geometric invariants was not found (30+ combinations 
   of modular forms, η-functions, and α_G tested).
3. Moduli τ_u, τ_d are not stabilized (simple KKLT does not 
   give a minimum at the required points).
4. The quark sector is not predictive (11 parameters for 9 
   independent observables). LOO errors reach 796% for |V_ub|.
5. θ_i is not predicted by the model — dark matter consistency 
   is achieved at a natural O(1) value, but this is not a 
   strict prediction.

We believe this transparency strengthens the scientific value 
of the work.

## Novelty and relevance

The main novelty is the strict derivation of 
**α_G = 2/(3π)** from the geometry of T²/ℤ₃ and the observation 
that the corresponding six-dimensional scale M₆ coincides with 
the GUT scale. This connects 6D geometry, modular symmetry, 
and the GUT scale in a non-trivial way.

The model makes testable predictions for upcoming experiments:
